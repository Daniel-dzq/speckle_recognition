# Competition / manuscript figure text — length experiment (Figure 4)

This note locks terminology for the **side-polished plastic fiber length sweep** so figures, captions, README, and reports stay consistent.

## Axis and naming (mandatory)

1. **Fiber9cm** means **total fiber length 9 cm** for that sample batch, not “9 cm green propagation” or “9 cm optimal green path”.
2. **Figure 4** horizontal axis: **Total fiber length (cm)** with nominal ticks **8, 9, 11, 13, 16** (groups Fiber8cm … Fiber16cm).
3. If a data table has **`green_prop_mm`**, treat it as **auxiliary side-polish geometry only**. Do **not** use it as the primary x-axis definition for Figure 4.
4. Do **not** state in body text or captions that “green propagation 9 cm is optimal”. The optimal **batch** is the **9 cm total-length** group under the combined criteria below.
5. Replace any legacy line such as “Fiber9cm corresponds to 2 cm green propagation” with:

   *The naming Fiber9cm refers to the total fiber length. The side-polished region is located at a fixed geometry in the setup and should not be confused with the total-length label.*

6. Use **类间/类内距离比** / **inter/intra distance ratio** everywhere. Do **not** use “类内/类间比” or inverted wording. For the selected standard length (paper `length_optimization_green` run), **inter/intra distance ratio = 1.5653**.

## Figure 4 — Chinese caption (final)

图 4 不同总长度侧抛塑料光纤的性能对比。（a）红光与绿光通道传输损耗随光纤总长度的变化；（b）类内距离、类间距离及类间/类内距离比随光纤总长度的变化，右轴为类间/类内距离比；（c）输出散斑像素熵随光纤总长度的变化。结果表明，总长度 9 cm 的光纤样本在绿光损耗 28.39 dB、类间/类内距离比 1.5653、像素熵 6.183 bit 的综合条件下表现最优，因此选为后续实验标准长度。

## Figure 4 — suggested Chinese body (final)

为确定侧抛塑料光纤的最佳总长度，本项目制备了总长度分别为 8、9、11、13、16 cm 的五组样本。通过综合比较传输损耗、类间/类内距离比和像素熵三项指标，结果表明，总长度 9 cm 的光纤在保持可接受绿光传输损耗（28.39 dB）的同时，实现了最高的类间/类内距离比（1.5653）和接近饱和的像素熵（6.183 bit）。因此，本文选定总长度 9 cm 的侧抛塑料光纤作为后续双通道激励与认证实验的标准样本。
