## 3. Publication pack pitfalls (do **not** substitute for 策划书 Fig. 4)

- `figures_publication/publication_fig03_length_optimization.*` and `publication_fig04_length_optimization.*` are **four-panel** exports from `scripts/make_publication_figures.py::fig03_length_optimization`.
- Data source is still **`length_optimization_green`** CSV when present, but **layout** (montage + separate ratio panel) conflicts with the user’s triple-panel Fig. 4 spec and invites caption mistakes.
- **PNG:** `publication_fig03_length_optimization.png` and `publication_fig04_length_optimization.png` are **bit-identical** (same SHA-256) — duplicate raster.
- **SVG/PDF:** hashes **differ** between fig03 and fig04 names (likely distinct export metadata), but they represent the **same figure family** — treat as paired naming clutter.

**Naming collision warning:** `publication_fig04_cross_fiber_auth` is a **different** figure (authentication). Do not confuse with `publication_fig04_length_optimization`.
