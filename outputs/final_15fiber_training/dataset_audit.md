## Split strategy (training)

Default final training uses **uniform_temporal**: all clips from each video are listed in time order, clip indices are shuffled with a fixed seed, then partitioned 70% train / 15% val / 15% test so splits sample the full timeline.

Legacy **contiguous_temporal** (first 70% / next 15% / last 15% of frames) remains available via `--split_strategy contiguous_temporal`.

Clips use disjoint frame ranges; the same video may contribute clips to multiple splits. Per-fiber `split_report.json` records clip indices per video.
